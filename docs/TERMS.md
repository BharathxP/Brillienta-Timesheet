came with your licence key. Use of the app is subject to that agreement and to your organisation's own acceptable-use
policies.

- The app is provided to run inside your Microsoft 365 tenant; your organisation remains the controller of all data
  stored in it (see the [Privacy statement](PRIVACY.md)).

  stops until an administrator enters a valid key; your data stays in your SharePoint lists. People beyond the plan's
  user limit can fill in timesheets but not submit them.
